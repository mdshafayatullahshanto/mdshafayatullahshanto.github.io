Solar Analysis – Scandex Textile Industries Ltd

Overview

This project involved conducting a comprehensive solar radiation analysis for the Scandex Textile Industries Ltd building, a 10-story commercial facility under the Panam Group. The analysis was performed using Autodesk Insight within Autodesk Revit to evaluate solar exposure across all building surfaces and floor levels.

The primary objective was to identify high solar gain zones (rooftop and facade), optimize energy efficiency strategies, and support informed decisions on façade design, shading, glazing placement and HVAC load calculations. The full BIM model — spanning Basement through Level 8 with structural, duct and electrical plans — was developed in Revit and used as the basis for the solar simulation.

Key Features

  • Full building solar radiation analysis across all 10 floors (Basement to Level 8) using Autodesk Insight
  • Per-floor solar heatmap generation — plan view color mapping from low (purple) to high (yellow) solar gain
  • Complete 3D BIM model built in Autodesk Revit including architectural, MEP duct and electrical systems
  • Rooftop analysis identified maximum solar exposure zones to support solar panel placement optimization
  • Facade solar exposure mapped to guide glazing selection, shading device design and HVAC load estimation
  • Energy performance benchmarking to support green building compliance and sustainability targets
  • Multi-discipline BIM coordination — structural plans, ceiling plans, duct layouts and electrical plans all integrated
  • Findings used to optimize building envelope and reduce cooling energy demand for the textile facility

Technical Components

  • BIM Model (Revit – Scandex Textile Industries Ltd): Full 10-story building model including Basement, Ground Floor, Levels 1–8; structural plans, ceiling plans, and 3D views coordinated across disciplines
  • Solar Analysis Tool: Autodesk Insight integrated within Revit — used to simulate annual and seasonal solar radiation on all building surfaces
  • Radiation Heatmap (3D): Isometric color-coded radiation map of the full building showing yellow (high solar gain) and purple (low/shaded) zones per floor slab and façade panel
  • Radiation Heatmap (Plan View): Floor-level plan heatmaps for each level showing solar intensity distribution across the open-plan floor plate, identifying columns, cores and perimeter edge effects
  • MEP Duct Layout: Per-floor duct plans (Ground Floor Duct through Level 6 Duct) modeled in Revit to coordinate HVAC system routing with solar-affected zones
  • Electrical Plans: Per-floor electrical layouts (Ground Floor Electrical through Level 6 Electrical) integrated in the same BIM environment
  • Building Elevation Model: Detailed architectural elevation showing multi-story façade with window patterns, louvres, and rooftop plant — used as input geometry for solar simulation
  • Client: Scandex Textile Industries Ltd, under Panam Group — 10-story commercial/industrial building; model date March 2024
