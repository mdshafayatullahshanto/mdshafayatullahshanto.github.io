Double Leaf Door – 3500×4000×200

Overview

The Double Leaf Door is a large-scale industrial access door designed and manufactured by JTK Power (Metalli-Jokela Oy). It features a robust split-frame construction with two independently hinged door leaves, engineered for heavy-duty industrial environments where wide clear openings and structural rigidity are critical.

The door is equipped with a pneumatic backup storage system, vibration supports, latch beams, and precision-machined hinges (Saranas). The surface is treated with Primer Temaprimer EUR at 80 µm. The complete assembly was modeled in 3D CAD (SolidWorks) and documented to scale A2 at 1:30.

Key Features

  • Double leaf (two-panel) configuration providing a clear opening width of 3,500 mm and height of 4,000 mm
  • Split frame design allowing modular assembly and installation in confined spaces
  • Pneumatic backup storage tank mounted on frame for reliable pneumatic-assisted operation
  • Latch beam system for secure closure and alignment of both door leaves
  • Leaves open to 90° — fully clear opening with no obstruction to passage
  • Overall assembly depth of 200 mm; total frame span 4,565 mm height × 3,820 mm width
  • Surface treatment: Primer Temaprimer EUR, 80 µm for corrosion protection
  • Fully detailed engineering drawing (Drawing No. 10VSE00003, Scale 1:30, Size A2)

Technical Components

  • Split Frame (8-E00003_3): Main structural perimeter frame split for modular transport and installation; qty 1
  • Right-Hand Door (8-E00004_3): Full-height right leaf panel with integrated hinge and latch interfaces; qty 1
  • Left-Hand Door (8-E00005_3): Full-height left leaf panel, mirror configuration to right-hand door; qty 1
  • Sarana / Hinges (8-59864 & 8-59863): Heavy-duty pivot hinges for each door leaf; qty 2 each side
  • Latch Beam (8-E00006_3): Cross-member locking beam to secure both leaves in closed position; qty 2
  • Salpalevy / Latch Plate (8-39496): Strike plate for latch engagement; qty 6
  • Vibration Support (8-53996): Damping supports to isolate door frame from structural vibration; qty 14
  • Pneumatic Backup Storage (8-60105): Pressurized reservoir tank mounted on door frame to maintain pneumatic operation during supply interruption; qty 1, weight 126.13 kg
  • Oven Opening Mechanism (8-60086): Pneumatic actuator assembly driving door leaf opening and closing; qty 2
  • Suojalevy / Guard Plate (2-60160): Protective steel cover plates (2×240×550 mm, S355K2+N EN10025-2); qty 4
  • Lukituslevy / Lock Plate (2-40076): Locking plates (5×50×5 mm, S235JR EN10025-2); qty 12
  • Aluslevy / Base Plates: M8 and M20 shim/base plates (DIN 125 Zn); used for alignment and fastening at multiple points
