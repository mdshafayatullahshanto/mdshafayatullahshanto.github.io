FDO A0(A60) Round Fire and Gas Damper

Overview

Halton FDO is a type-approved class A0(A60) round fire and gas damper designed for use in marine and offshore ventilation systems. It can be installed in circular ducts and prevents the spread of fire through ventilation ductwork while maintaining minimal pressure loss during normal operation.

All FDO fire dampers incorporate a fusible link mechanism. When the blade is in the open position, the device causes no significant pressure loss, noise, or flow disturbance. Dampers can be set from outside and installed in any position, with an open/closed indicator visible externally.

Key Features

  • Type-approved by the most recognized classification societies — rated class A0 without insulation, A15–A60 when suitably insulated
  • Designed for circular duct installation — size range Ø100–500 mm; sizes from Ø160 available in 1 mm increments
  • Blade seals effective up to 270°C; fusible link nominal release at 50°C, 74°C, or 100°C — other temperatures available on request
  • Very low leakage; maximum duct pressure 5,000 Pa and maximum air velocity 15 m/s
  • Flanges and drilling available as an option per ISO 15138 standards; special flanges available on request
  • Available as ATEX / IECEx certified; SIL 2 safety assessment certificate available on specific terms
  • Open/closed indicator visible from outside; can be set and installed in any orientation

Technical Components

  • Frame: Carbon steel (painted/galvanized) or stainless steel EN 1.4301 / 1.4404 / 1.4432 — 3 mm standard thickness or per SOLAS; sizes Ø100 and Ø125 not available in stainless steel
  • Blades (Flap): Two 1 mm galvanized or stainless steel sheets riveted together, with maintenance-free AISI316L stainless steel bearings and shafts
  • FDO-EL: Electrical spring-return actuator (24V / 120V / 230V) with built-in open/closed limit switches and a separate junction box — fusible link cuts voltage and spring closes blades; auto-reopens when fuse is replaced and voltage restored. Wide range of Ex actuators available including a one-second closing time option
  • FDO-PNR: Pneumatic rotating actuator — fusible link cuts air pressure and springs close blades; auto-reopens when fuse is replaced and pneumatic supply is restored
  • FDO-SP: Manual spring-actuated damper with fusible link — requires manual reset to open position after a fire event
  • DOT: Manual override function available for PNR and EL models
  • HSO (Halton Smart Override): HVAC black-start function available for EL and PNR models — automatically resets when power or pneumatic air supply is reinstated
