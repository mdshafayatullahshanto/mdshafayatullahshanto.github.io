UTP – Balancing Damper

Overview

Halton UTP balancing dampers are used to balance airflow rates in high-pressure ductwork systems. Designed for offshore, marine and industrial applications, the UTP serves as a reliable balancing damper where accurate airflow control is critical.

In the open position, the blades face the direction of flow and do not cause significant pressure loss. The UTP features a double-sheet blade construction with maintenance-free stainless steel bearings and shafts, and is available with electrical, pneumatic or manual actuation — including the Halton Smart Override (HSO) black-start function for EL and PNR models.

Key Features

  • Used for balancing air intake and exhaust in high-pressure ductwork systems
  • Shock tested for offshore and marine environments
  • Leakage class of a closed damper up to Class 1 per EN1751:2014 (tested size 1000×1000 mm); casing leakage class B
  • Frame of galvanized, painted or stainless steel; blades of galvanized or stainless steel with double-sheet construction; maintenance-free stainless steel bearings and shafts
  • Electrical, pneumatic or manual operation system available
  • Connection pieces for round duct available
  • Maximum duct pressure 5,000 Pa and maximum air velocity 15 m/s
  • Operating temperature range up to +100°C; optionally up to +180°C
  • Available as ATEX certified; SIL 2 safety assessment certificate available on specific terms

Technical Components

  • Frame: Carbon steel (painted/galvanized) or stainless steel EN 1.4301 (AISI304) / EN 1.4404 (AISI316L) / EN 1.4432 (AISI316L) — frame thickness from 3 mm to 10 mm; standard 3 mm
  • Blades: Two 1 mm sheets in sandwich design — galvanized steel or stainless steel EN 1.4301 / EN 1.4404 / EN 1.4432; maintenance-free stainless steel bearings and shafts EN 1.4404 (AISI316L)
  • Bearings & Shafts: Maintenance-free stainless steel EN 1.4404 (AISI316L); bronze (JM5) or brass options available
  • UTP-EL: Electrical spring-return actuator — standard 24 VAC/DC, 230 VAC or 120 VAC; built-in open/closed limit switches depending on actuator choice; wide range of Ex-rated actuators available including 1-second closing time option
  • UTP-PNR: Pneumatic rotating actuator — Air Torque AT100 or AT200 series; stainless steel (AISI316) versions available
  • UTP-MAN: Manual handle operation for applications where motorized actuation is not required
  • HSO (Halton Smart Override): HVAC black-start function available for EL and PNR models — automatically resets when power or pneumatic air supply is reinstated
  • Size Range: Rectangular ducts width 100–1200 mm × height 100–1600 mm; circular Ø100–1250 mm; modular constructions up to 2400×3200 mm; standard flange width 27 mm; ISO 15138 flange drilling available
