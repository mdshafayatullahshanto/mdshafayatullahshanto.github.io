BRD – Pressure-Relief Damper

Overview

Halton BRD pressure-relief dampers are used in offshore, marine and industrial applications to regulate the pressure in the ductwork system. Operating entirely without an actuator or motor, the BRD relies on imbalanced blades to provide automatic one-way pressure relief.

When duct pressure rises above the selected threshold, the blades open to relieve excess pressure; when pressure drops below the minimum opening force, the blades automatically close and seal the duct. The opening pressure is easily adjusted by repositioning the counterweights, and final fine-tuning is carried out on-site during commissioning.

Key Features

  • Fixed frame in painted, galvanized or stainless steel; blades of galvanized or stainless steel
  • Models available for both horizontal and vertical installation
  • Blades contain silicone seal to lower leakage; leakage class up to Class 2 per EN1751:2014
  • Available as ATEX / IECEx certified
  • Blades linked and open in parallel for uniform pressure relief
  • Opening pressure adjustable by repositioning counterweights — standard on right-hand side; left-hand side available on request
  • Opening pressure adjustable from 30 Pa up to 300 Pa; final adjustment done during on-site commissioning
  • Maximum duct pressure 5,000 Pa and maximum air velocity 15 m/s
  • Operating temperature range up to +100°C; optionally up to +180°C

Technical Components

  • Frame: Carbon steel (painted/galvanized) or stainless steel EN 1.4301 (AISI304) / EN 1.4404 (AISI316L) — standard thickness 3 mm
  • Blades: Two 0.8 mm sheets in sandwich design — galvanized steel or stainless steel EN 1.4301 / EN 1.4404; fitted with silicone seals to minimize leakage
  • Bearings & Shafts: Maintenance-free stainless steel EN 1.4404 (AISI316L) bearings and shafts; bronze bearings available as an option
  • Operation Principle: Imbalanced blades allow one-way pressure relief; blades open when duct pressure exceeds the set threshold (30–300 Pa) and close automatically when pressure drops below the minimum opening force
  • Counterweights: Included as standard — placed on right-hand side; left-hand side available as option; final adjustment performed during commissioning on-site
  • Connection Options: Rectangular and circular (D1, D1 outlet, D2); ISO 15138 flange drilling available; standard flange width 27 mm
  • Size Range: Rectangular ducts width 150–1200 mm × height 150–1400 mm; circular Ø100–1250 mm; modular constructions up to 2400×2800 mm
