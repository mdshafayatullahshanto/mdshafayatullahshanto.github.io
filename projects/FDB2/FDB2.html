FDB2 A0(A60) Fire and Gas Damper

Overview

Halton FDB2 is the world's most widely sold marine fire and gas damper, designed for offshore, marine, and navy ventilation systems. It prevents the spread of fire and gases through ventilation ductwork while maintaining minimal pressure loss during normal operation.

The damper combines a serviceed double-skin blade structure with dual sealing technology — silicone seals for everyday low-leakage performance and graphite expansion seals that activate in fire conditions, increasing tightness by up to 50%.

Key Features

  • Type-approved by the most recognized classification societies — rated class A0 without insulation, A15–A60 when suitably insulated
  • Shock and vibration tested for offshore and marine environments
  • Dual seal system: silicone seals (effective up to 300°C) for normal conditions; graphite thermal expansion seals (from 150°C) increase tightness up to 50% in a fire event
  • Blade leakage class 1–3 per EN1751:2014 depending on size; casing leakage class B
  • Fusible link release at nominal 50°C, 74°C, or 100°C — other temperatures available on request
  • Low weight due to serviceed double-skin blade structure (1 mm sandwich sheets)
  • Available as ATEX / IECEx certified; SIL 2 safety assessment certificate available on specific terms

Technical Components

  • Frame: Carbon steel (painted/galvanized) or stainless steel EN 1.4301/1.4404/1.4432 — 3–5 mm thickness per SOLAS, up to 10 mm on request
  • Blades: Two 1 mm galvanized or stainless steel sheets in sandwich design with maintenance-free AISI316L stainless steel bearings and shafts
  • FDB2-EL: Electrical spring-return actuator (24V / 120V / 230V) with built-in open/closed limit switches — fusible link cuts voltage and spring closes blades; auto-reopens when fuse is replaced and voltage restored
  • FDB2-PNR: Pneumatic rotating actuator — fusible link cuts air pressure and springs close blades; auto-reopens when fuse replaced and pneumatic supply restored
  • FDB2-SP: Manual spring-actuated damper with fusible link — requires manual reset to open position after a fire event
  • HSO (Halton Smart Override): HVAC black-start function available for EL and PNR models — automatically resets when power or pneumatic air supply is reinstated
  • Size range: Rectangular ducts 100–1200 × 100–1600 mm; circular Ø100–1250 mm; modular constructions available for larger sizes
